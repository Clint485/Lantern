# Lantern reset: 12-month action plan

**Action-plan period:** 27 November 2026–30 November 2027. This plan assumes the Committee adopts the reset after its 26 November decision and that the Card 3 regulatory intervention occurs in January 2027. The Gantt also shows the Sprint 40 baseline start so the Sprint 54 date is visible.

## Cost and schedule basis

- Castellan's acceleration offer forecasts Tower practical completion by **31 March 2027**. The case materials do not state its incremental cost. Obtain a priced, contract-checked proposal before approving expenditure at Gate 1.
- The **$14 million** estimate is for 40 Flow contractors for six months under the “hold the date” option; it is not the Tower acceleration price and is not assumed in this plan.
- Keep the baseline **three-month commissioning period (April–June 2027)** and **ten-week Flow cutover (17 April–25 June 2027)** unless Gate 1 approves a costed, evidence-based alternative. Required safety, clinical and commissioning checks are not removed to claim time savings.
- The case estimates parallel running at **$2.1 million per month** if Tower and Flow are decoupled. The **$21 million** saving from replacing the heat pumps and rooftop solar would break the net-zero and Green Star commitments and is not recommended.
- The case states **$38 million of uncommitted contingency** remains. Gate 1 should compare the full cost of approved measures with that amount and identify any funding decision needed.

| Proposal / exposure | Cost stated in the case | Gate 1 budgeting treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Tower acceleration to 31 March | Not stated | Obtain a priced, contract-checked proposal from Castellan / Meridian. |
| 40 Flow contractors for six months | $14 million | Comparator only; require evidence that a targeted resource option is likely to produce a net time saving. |
| Commissioning compression | Not stated | Price any proposed parallel preparation or testing; retain the three-month window unless the certifier supports a safe alternative. |
| Squad resources and clinical backfill | Not stated | Estimate resource, induction, backfill and supervision costs; pilot or validate with squad members before scaling. |
| Face-to-face training and community sessions | Not stated | Estimate staff release, casual backfill, travel and facilitation costs. |
| Parallel Tower / Flow operations if decoupled | $2.1 million per month | Include as a contingency exposure if the staged plan needs parallel running. |
